How this works

Grace period
After an installment due date, members still have this many days to pay without late-payment interest. Late interest starts the day after the grace period ends.
Example
Due date August 1 with 5 grace days: payment through August 6 has no late interest. Late payment applies starting August 7.
Zero days
Set to 0 to apply late interest starting the day after the due date (same as before this setting existed).
Minimum membership
New members cannot request a loan until they have been registered for this many months. Set to 0 to allow requests immediately.
One-person approval
When enabled, any one admin, loan officer, staff, or credit committee member can approve or reject at the committee step. Turn off to require a majority of listed approvers again.
