Tips

Term (months)
How many monthly payments the loan is split into. Term does not change interest — only the installment count.
Interest rate
Set on each loan application (Apply step), not on the product — so rates can differ per borrower.
Late interest from month
First installment month that can receive late interest if unpaid past due (1 = first month).
Usable days
Interest day count is set on Apply from a From/To date range — not on the product.
Amount range
Applications outside min/max are rejected at entry.
