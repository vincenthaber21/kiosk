Credit Interest Settings

Set the interest rate and grace period for unpaid credit (utang). Members can pay within the grace period with no interest. After that, wait one full month, then each unpaid month adds principal × rate. Example (principal ₱500, rate 0.015, grace 2 days): first charge after grace + 1 month → ₱507.50; next month → ₱507.50 + ₱7.50 = ₱515.00. Set the rate to 0 to turn interest off.
