What this page shows

Registered members have an active Senior or PWD profile in Members — they receive senior/PWD segment fixed-peso rules at checkout. The table lists only members who used segment or manual discounts in the selected period. The walk-in table lists only non-member buyers who received kiosk Discount ₱ in the selected period. Totals use completed and partially refunded sales; voided/refunded lines are excluded. Automatic promos and segment rules are applied to unit price before the line is saved.
